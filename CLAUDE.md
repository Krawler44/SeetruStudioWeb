## Known Content Weaknesses (vs Work.co benchmark)
1. Hero headline is identity-focused ("We Are A...") not outcome-focused
2. No client testimonials anywhere on the site
3. Case studies show deliverables, not business outcomes or metrics
4. No process/methodology section ("How We Work")
5. No team bios or founder narrative
6. CTA path jumps straight to contact — no intermediate trust-building
7. Blog/thought leadership last updated 2022
8. No explicit audience definition or target vertical
9. Stats quote ("Discover sophisticated workarounds...") is weak and forgettable
10. Awards section exists but is not prominently featured

## Content Priorities for V2
- P1: Outcome-focused hero headline
- P1: 3–5 client testimonials with name and role
- P1: Metrics added to case studies (engagement lift, traffic, completion rate)
- P2: "How We Work" process section (3–4 steps)
- P2: Team/founder story with credentials
- P2: Layered CTA path (explore work → read case study → get in touch)
- P3: Revive blog with 1 post/month minimum
- P3: Define and name the target client explicitly in hero or about copy
